Annual Financial Report

Fiscal Year 2026 — Confidential

1. Executive Summary

This report provides stakeholders with a detailed analysis of our financial performance throughout fiscal year 2026. Strategic initiatives have yielded significant growth across all major business segments.

Revenue increased by 18.7% year-over-year, driven by expansion in emerging markets and successful product launches. Operating margins improved to 24.3%, reflecting continued focus on operational efficiency.

2. Revenue Analysis

Total consolidated revenue reached $4.82 billion for the fiscal year, exceeding analyst expectations by 6.2%. Breakdown by segment:

• Enterprise Solutions: $2.14B (+22.1% YoY)
• Cloud Services: $1.68B (+31.4% YoY)
• Professional Services: $0.72B (+8.3% YoY)
• Licensing: $0.28B (+4.6% YoY)
